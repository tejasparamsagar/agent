Registration Process for HT Code Hub Programs

How to Register:

1. Visit Our Website
Go to https://learning.heuristictechpark.com/ and scroll to the registration section on the homepage.

2. Fill Out the Registration Form
The registration form requires:
- Complete name (your full name)
- Email address (for confirmation and communication)
- Phone number (for direct contact)
- Select Program (choose from the dropdown):
  * Full-Stack Builder Program
  * Data Science Builder Program
  * Data Analyst Builder Program
  * AI/ML Builder Program

3. Agree to Terms
You must read and agree to HTCodeHub's Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions before submitting.

4. Submit the Form
Click the "REGISTER" button to submit your registration.

5. Confirmation
You will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours after submission. Our team will then contact you to complete the enrollment process.

What Happens After Registration:
- You receive a confirmation email within 24 hours
- Our team contacts you to discuss your chosen program
- We help you understand the program details
- You complete the enrollment process
- You get access to course materials and start your learning journey

Registration Requirements:
- Your information is required to complete the registration
- It's safe with us and will not be used for marketing
- You will receive a confirmation email in less than 24 hours

Privacy and Security:
We take your privacy seriously. Your information is safe with us and will not be used for marketing purposes. We only use your information to:
- Process your registration
- Send you confirmation emails
- Contact you about your chosen program
- Provide you with course information
